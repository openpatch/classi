Classi is a local-first app for teachers. All your class data stays on your device, encrypted and under your control — no cloud account required.

Your data is stored in encrypted .classi libraries kept in the app's own storage. A portable .classi-backup file can be exported automatically to a WebDAV server whenever the app locks or you switch libraries, making sync between devices straightforward.

<b>Features</b>
• Groups and student lists with archive, clone, and batch creation
• WebUntis class-list CSV import
• Grade entry with configurable grading systems and chart-based history
• Checklists with per-student links
• Notes for groups and individual students
• Homework and material tracking
• Biometric unlock (fingerprint / face) in addition to passphrase
• Auto-export backups to a folder of your choice
• English and German interface

<b>Privacy</b>
Classi stores all data locally on your device. No analytics, no tracking, no network access required.
