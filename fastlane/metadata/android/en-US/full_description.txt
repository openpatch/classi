Classi is a local-first app for teachers. All your class data stays on your device, encrypted and under your control — no cloud account required.

Your data is stored in encrypted .classi libraries kept in the app's own storage. A portable .classi-backup file can be exported automatically to a WebDAV server of your own whenever the app locks or you switch libraries, and imported again on your other devices, which keeps them in sync.

<b>Features</b>
• Groups and student lists with archive, clone, and batch creation
• WebUntis class-list CSV import
• Grade entry with configurable grading systems and chart-based history
• Lesson mode for attendance, homework, and material checks
• Weekly timetable and lesson planning across the school year
• Seating plans for each group
• Checklists with per-student links
• Notes for groups and individual students
• Student-designed avatars
• Biometric unlock (fingerprint / face) in addition to passphrase
• Automatic backup export and import over WebDAV
• English and German interface

<b>Privacy</b>
Classi stores all data locally on your device. No analytics, no tracking, no account. The only server Classi talks to is the WebDAV server you set up yourself.
